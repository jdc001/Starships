# Starships (working title)

A one-on-one starship duel game for iPhone and iPad, played in simultaneous turns.

**Play:** https://jdc001.github.io/starships/

On iPhone, open the link in Safari and choose Share > Add to Home Screen to play it full screen.

## How it plays

Each turn has two halves:

1. **Plan.** Time is frozen. Plot a course by tapping or dragging in space, or pick a tactical order (Strafe, Orbit, Lance, Evade, About, Stop). The dotted line shows exactly where your ship will go, lit up where your weapons will bear. Arm or hold each weapon and queue modules.
2. **Engage.** Both ships carry out their orders together for six seconds. The Kesh plan in secret at the same time.

Between battles, take missions from the board, earn credits, upgrade your ship in the shipyard or trade it in for a bigger hull. Progress saves in the browser.
